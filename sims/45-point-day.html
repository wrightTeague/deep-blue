      Players other than the captains and president use stand-in names (W1, M1…), numbered from most practices to fewest, so made-up attendance isn't pinned on a real person. The dots after each name are practices made out of 6.

      Other ideas, not simulated yet

      • Pods. Fixed groups of 3–4 (handler pods and cutter pods) sent out in combinations, like "Pod A + Pod D". Easy to call from the sideline, people learn to play together, and the combinations still mix the team.
      • Change by game. Whole lines in the early games, when legs are fresh and rhythm matters more. Swap every point in the later games, when everyone is tired.
      • Separate rotations for women and men. Women and men each run on their own cycle. Most of the captains are men, so with 3 women on every line the other women end up playing a bit more than the other men. Separate cycles could even that out without needing the 5 men / 2 women lines.
